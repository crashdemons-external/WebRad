Engines & licenses

WebRad includes native signal-processing engines. They run in WebAssembly on your device. No audio, picture, file, or decoded message is sent to a server.

  • fldigi: GNU GPL
  • MMSSTV, QSSTV, OpenJPEG and WEFAX notices
  • Dire Wolf, ft8_lib, ggwave, multimon-ng, Codec2 / FreeDV and rtl_433 notices
  • minimodem serial tone detector: GPL-3.0-or-later
  • Matthew Kaufman MDC-1200 and FleetSync: GPL-2.0-only
  • aptdec satellite DSP: GPL-2.0-or-later
  • WSPR decoder and channel encoder: GPL-3.0-or-later
  • acarsdec notices, Library GPL 2.0 core headers and upstream GPL 2.0 license
  • KISS FFT: BSD license, reused for serial tones, WSPR and WebRad's spectrogram engine

Source code and license notices are included with the project and in the deployed licenses/ directory.

Some native modes require hardware, external models, or host services outside the supplied browser engines. The format catalog lists compiled capabilities; receiver performance on noisy real-world signals depends on the recording and selected setup.
